Caveat on interpretation: with n = 915,067 the test is enormously

actually changes materially over follow-up -- not on the p-value.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
REMEDIATED MODEL (stratification + time-varying coefficients)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Strategy:
  * plan_length and payment_method are the largest PH violations and
    are nuisance/design variables rather than the effects of interest,
    so they go into strata(): each gets its own baseline hazard shape
    and no proportionality is imposed on them.
  * auto_renew, city_grp and reg_channel get TIME-VARYING coefficients:
    follow-up is split at 30 and 180 days and each covariate is
    interacted with the resulting period, so its HR may differ by period.
  * Idiom is Therneau's `x:strata(tgroup)` on survSplit output, which
    avoids the non-estimable period main effect (within a risk set at
    time t, every subject is in the same period).

survSplit: 915,067 spells -> 1,858,506 person-period rows at cuts 30, 180
